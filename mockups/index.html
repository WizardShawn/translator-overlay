Translator Overlay · UI mockups · 1 Oct 2026

Play any game in your own language, without touching its files.

A click-through window sits over the game, reads the text on screen, and paints the translation where the original was. These mockups walk through the app in the order you would meet it. The game shown is invented for the mockups, with original art and text.

What happens to every new line of text

On your PCCaptureGrab the game window, minus the overlay itself.~5 ms
On your PCFind textWindows OCR boxes each text region.~40 ms
On your PCWait until stillTypewriter text finishes before anything is sent.200 ms
On your PCCheck the cacheMenus and seen lines paint instantly.~2 ms
ModelTranslateCrops + profile + recent dialogue, streamed back.~1.2 s
On your PCPaintPatch in the sampled colour, text fitted to the box.~8 ms
